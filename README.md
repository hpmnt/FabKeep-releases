# FabKeep

**AI-powered maker file management for Windows.**

FabKeep keeps your maker files organised — like a music library, but for models and projects. It finds the files in your folders, shows a preview of each one, lets you sort them into categories, and opens each file in its own program with one click.

> **Open beta.** FabKeep is free and in open beta: almost everything works, but you may meet rough edges. Please report anything odd — see [Feedback and Security](Feedback-and-Security).

## What FabKeep does

- **One library for all your maker files** — 3D prints (3MF, STL, G-code), CNC projects (VCarve, Aspire, Carveco, Carbide Create, toolpaths), LightBurn laser projects, Cricut cut files, CAD models (Fusion 360, FreeCAD, STEP, Blender) and vector art (SVG, DXF, EPS, AI). See [Supported files and programs](Supported-Files-and-Programs).
- **Previews of everything** — the picture stored in the file, or one FabKeep draws itself, so you recognise a project at a glance.
- **Live 3D preview** — turn, zoom and inspect models on your printer's build plate, with real filament colours and painted colours from Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer projects, multi-plate projects plate by plate, and a large view on double-click. CNC toolpaths are shown carved into a block of wood.
- **"Does it fit?"** — every model, job and drawing is checked against your 3D printers, CNC routers, lasers and Cricut machines.
- **Categories, tags, notes and favourites** — sort files your way; search finds them by name, tag, note or category.
- **Opens each file in the right program** — your slicer for prints, LightBurn for laser projects, Vectric for `.crv`, Cricut Design Space for cut files, and so on.
- **Printers & machines** — your machines with their build volumes and work areas, the filaments you use, and maintenance reminders with a log.
- **Optional AI features** (off until you switch them on):
  - **AI sorting** suggests categories for your files — with an AI on your own PC (free and private) or a cloud AI. See [Optional AI features](Optional-AI-Features).
  - **3D design** — describe a part in words and an AI builds it in Fusion 360 and saves an STL into your library.
- **Updates itself** — new versions are found and installed automatically.

## Your files are safe

FabKeep only **reads** your model files. It never renames, moves, changes or deletes them — removing a file or folder from FabKeep only removes it from the library. FabKeep has no server of its own and uploads nothing; the only time file information leaves your PC is if you choose a cloud AI for the optional AI features.
