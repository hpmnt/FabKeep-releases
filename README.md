# FabKeep

**AI-powered maker file management for Windows.**

FabKeep keeps your maker files organised — like a music library, but for models and projects. It finds the files in your folders, shows a preview of each one, lets you sort them into categories, and opens each file in its own program with one click.

> **Open beta.** FabKeep is free and in open beta: almost everything works, but you may meet rough edges. Please report anything odd — see [Feedback and Security](https://github.com/hpmnt/FabKeep-releases/wiki/Feedback-and-Security).

## What FabKeep does

- **One library for all your maker files** — 3D prints (3MF, STL, G-code), CNC projects (VCarve, Aspire, Carveco, Carbide Create, toolpaths), LightBurn laser projects, Cricut cut files, CAD models (Fusion 360, FreeCAD, STEP, Blender) and vector art (SVG, DXF, EPS, AI). See [Supported files and programs](https://github.com/hpmnt/FabKeep-releases/wiki/Supported-Files-and-Programs).
- **Previews of everything** — the picture stored in the file, or one FabKeep draws itself, so you recognise a project at a glance.
- **Live 3D preview** — turn, zoom and inspect models on your printer's build plate, with real filament colours and painted colours from Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer projects, multi-plate projects plate by plate, and a large view on double-click. CNC toolpaths are shown carved into a block of wood.
- **"Does it fit?"** — every model, job and drawing is checked against your 3D printers, CNC routers, lasers and Cricut machines.
- **Categories, tags, notes and favourites** — sort files your way; search finds them by name, tag, note or category.
- **Opens each file in the right program** — your slicer for prints, LightBurn for laser projects, Vectric for `.crv`, Cricut Design Space for cut files, and so on.
- **Zip downloads** — see what is inside a zip, its model in 3D, and unpack it with one click (**Extract here**).
- **Workshop log**: a **To make** list, and **I made this** with date, machine, material, a note and a photo. FabKeep also keeps the settings a print was sliced with and shows **what worked** last time.
- **Orders, materials and an overview**: log what you sold, with fees, costs, profit and the sales tax you owe; keep track of filament, sheets and vinyl on hand; and see what you made, sold and need to do at a glance. See [Workshop](https://github.com/hpmnt/FabKeep-releases/wiki/Workshop).
- **Price lists**: a printable PDF of your models with pictures, sizes and prices, for a shop or a craft fair.
- **Print cost and selling prices** — filament, electricity, machine wear and failed prints, then the price to charge on Etsy, eBay, Amazon Handmade, your own site or at a craft fair, after their fees, with your local sales tax handled (kept up to date automatically where the official data is free). See [Costs and Selling](https://github.com/hpmnt/FabKeep-releases/wiki/Costs-and-Selling).
- **Designer & license** — who made a design and whether you may sell what you make from it (found by itself in MakerWorld files and the LICENSE / README notes that come with downloads).
- **Mesh check**: models are checked for holes, broken edges and flipped faces before you slice them.
- **Duplicates, filters and saved views**: find files you have twice; filter by what fits a machine, size, made / not made, license or mesh problems; keep a search or set of filters in the sidebar.
- **Downloads inbox** (optional): new model files and zips in your Downloads folder go straight into your library, with zips unpacked.
- **Automatic backups** — every day, week or month into a folder you choose (e.g. OneDrive).
- **Printers & machines** — 3D printers (Bambu Lab, QIDI, Prusa, Creality …), CNC routers, lasers and Cricut machines, with their build volumes and work areas, the filaments you use, and maintenance reminders with a log.
- **Optional AI features** (off until you switch them on):
  - **AI sorting** suggests categories for your files — with an AI on your own PC (free and private) or a cloud AI. See [Optional AI features](https://github.com/hpmnt/FabKeep-releases/wiki/Optional-AI-Features).
  - **3D design** — describe a part in words and an AI builds it in Fusion 360 and saves an STL into your library.
- **Updates itself**: new versions are found and installed automatically, and FabKeep shows **what's new** afterwards.
- **Crash reports, opt-in**: after a crash, FabKeep offers to open a filled-in bug report for you to submit. It never sends anything by itself.

## Your files are safe

FabKeep only **reads** your model files. It never renames, changes or deletes them, and removing a file or folder from FabKeep only removes it from the library. The one exception is the optional **Downloads inbox**: when you switch it on, it moves new downloads from your Downloads folder into the library folder you choose. FabKeep has no server of its own and uploads nothing; the only time file information leaves your PC is if you choose a cloud AI for the optional AI features.
